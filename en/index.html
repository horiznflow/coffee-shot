About

You come for the coffee.You stay for everything else.

At The Coffee Shot, the day starts with an espresso and ends with a cocktail. In between there are breakfasts, sandwiches, pizzas, desserts and fresh juices, with service our guests always mention. And on Fridays and Saturdays, we host themed nights with live DJs spinning vinyl.
